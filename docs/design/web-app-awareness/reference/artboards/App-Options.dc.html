6Interstitials, modals, nagsno
Full-screen "Open in the app" interstitial,
"Install our app" modal, nag on every visit.
Not drawn on purpose.
The web view is a first-class surface and must stay the fallback that always works — for MMS-stripped carriers, for friends on Android, for a laptop at the yacht club. Nothing that blocks the chart, nothing that repeats, nothing that asks twice. If someone said no once, the footer card is the only reminder they ever get.
Measure: taps on the header link, the footer card and the outing card as three events; the Smart Banner is Apple's and reports nothing. Expect most installs from 5.
