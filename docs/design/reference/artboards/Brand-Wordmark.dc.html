Rules
Where the name appears. iPhone: a 17 pt eyebrow lockup above the location on the main screen, the About screen title, nowhere else. Web: the header lockup (it replaces nothing — the location stays the h1). Watch: only the settings footer ("Wind Watch 1.0 · Weather data by Open-Meteo.com") and the inline complication's "Wind — open app". The watch app screens stay nameless on purpose: the icon in the dock and on the face is the brand there.
Clear space = the height of the "W" on all sides. Minimum size 13 pt (name only, no tile below 13).
The wake is the only coloured part of the brand. Three streaks in B2 / B3 / B4, always that order, always hard-stepped — the same colours as the bars, so the brand never introduces a colour the data doesn't use. The arrow is the same white as every chart arrow; the tile is ground ink.
Never: amber (means stale), B5+ colours in the wake, gradients, a cloud/sun/wave, the arrow pointing any direction other than 103° in the mark (it may rotate freely in charts — that's data, not brand), the wake without the arrow, more than three streaks, outlined or stroked wordmark, all-caps.
Voice in copy. Short, concrete, sailor-plain: "gusts to 25 overnight", "Couldn't reach server", "No forecast — open app". No exclamation marks, no "Hey!", no weather-app cheer. The name is the only thing with personality; the numbers stay all business.
Alt text / VoiceOver for the mark: "Wind Watch". The arrow in charts keeps its directional label; the brand arrow has none.
Chosen icon: B·3 "Wake". Lockup tiles use the size tiers automatically — 28 pt and up get two streaks, 18 pt and below get one. See "Brand · icon final".
