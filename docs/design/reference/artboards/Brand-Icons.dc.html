App icon — three directions, each built only from the chart's own marks · shown at 180 / 60 / 29 pt and as the round watch icon
A · Gust caps
Three bars climbing B2 → B3 → B4 with the gust line and tick caps over them. It is literally the chart, so the icon teaches the grammar before the app opens. Reads at 29 pt because the caps are the brightest thing. Most "app-like"; least distinctive from a distance among other data-app icons.
B · The arrow
One shape, the TO-arrow, at the bay's prevailing rotation (a westerly, blowing to 103°). Ground is Beaufort 4 green — the "good sailing day" step. Unmistakable at every size, survives tinted and monochrome home screens, and the exact same path is already in every chart. Risk: looks like a navigation/compass app until you know it.
C · Beaufort ring
The circular complication's ring, five ramp steps climbing clockwise and stopping — the "gauge that stops" is the honesty idea made into a mark — around a Rounded Heavy W. Most ownable of the three and the only one with a letterform, so it can carry the name alone. Busiest at 29 pt; the ring must drop to four steps there.
Decided: B, with the B·3 wake — see "Brand · icon final". Original note: On a wet wrist and in a dock full of icons the single shape wins; the arrow is also the one glyph already on every surface. Build it as an iOS 26 layered icon: green ground layer, arrow as the foreground glyph layer so Liquid Glass tint/clear modes keep the silhouette.
Icon ground tokens: A/C `#0B0E12` (ground), B `bft.4 #8DD44A`. Never the amber — amber means "stale" in this product and should never be decorative. Watch icon is the same art in a circle; the arrow shrinks 7 % so the kite's wings clear the edge.
Tinted / clear-glass mode: A becomes three grey bars + white caps (still legible), B is a solid silhouette (ideal), C loses its ramp and becomes a plain ring + W (fine). Dark mode home screen: all three already sit on near-black.
