Glyphs (all drawable as marks)
Arrow — kite shape, points TO (data FROM + 180°). Height 1.0 u, width 0.76 u, notch at 0.38 u. 9 pt phone/web chart, 14 pt watch, 28 pt hero.
Tick cap — horizontal line, bar width + 2 pt, same weight as gust line (1.5 pt; 2.5 pt watch), centred on bar at gust value. Gust line joins cap centres.
Sun markers — on the plot's top edge: sunrise = filled 6 pt amber dot, sunset = 6 pt amber ring (1.5 pt stroke, 0.75 pt smaller so they read the same size). "On after it rises, off after it sets." No rays, no icon.
Now marker — 1.5 pt white rule, full plot height + 6 pt overshoot, "now" label right of it at the top.
Horizon edge — 1 pt dashed (3/3) at 70 %, plot stops dead; label "HRRR ends 05:00" right-aligned to the edge. Empty slots stay empty.
