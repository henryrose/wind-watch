Two models, one rule
HRRR3 km, hourly, to ~45 h. The truth for the first two days. Already what every surface shows. NBMNational Blend of Models, 2.5 km over CONUS. Hourly to 36 h, 3-hourly to day 7, 6-hourly to day 10. A bias-corrected blend; smoother than HRRR by construction.
The rule. HRRR owns every hour it covers. NBM is drawn only after HRRR ends, and the switch is visible. NBM values for the near term are never shown, blended, overlaid, badged or hinted at — not in the chart, the table, the readout, the peak chip or the complications. Reason: for the same hour HRRR can say G31 and NBM G9; showing both undercuts the number the sailor is about to act on.
Fetching. Two requests, never a blended endpoint. Request NBM from the HRRR horizon onward only (`start_hour = hrrr.horizon + 1`), so the near-term NBM data isn't even on the device to leak. Aggregate NBM to 3-hour blocks (sustained = block max, gust = block max, direction = vector mean) even where it offers hourly; the switch should read as a change of step, not a change of mood.
Why not blend at the seam. A smoothed join would hide a real disagreement between models on the handoff hour. If the first NBM block jumps from the last HRRR bar, that jump is information. Leave it.
The switch — what changes at the seam
Seam ruleThe existing dashed HRRR-edge rule, full plot height. Label left HRRR, label right NBM · 3-hourly. Region tintNBM side gets a flat #4FA3D8 at 4.5 % over the plot. Lightness, not hue, so it survives greyscale. BarsSame Beaufort ramp, 3-hour slots, drawn at 70 % opacity. The ramp is still the magnitude; the opacity is the model. Gust lineSolid on HRRR, dashed 4/3 at 60 % on NBM. This is the cue that reads at any size — a dashed line is "forecast-ier" without a legend. Caps, arrowsUnchanged, so the grammar is one grammar. Readout / tooltipLabel becomes a span with a model tag: Sat 12–3pm · NBM. HRRR hours carry no tag; the absence is the default. Peak chipReads across the whole range; names the day and, if the peak is NBM, the model: peak G32 · Sat 1pm · NBM. TableHourly rows to the seam, then 3-hour rows labelled by span with a small NBM tag. A thin rule and a one-line header NBM from Fri 5am · 3-hourly mark the switch inside the table too.
Ranges. 48 h (default; HRRR only, exactly today's chart) and 7 d (HRRR to its horizon, then NBM to 168 h). NBM reaches 10 days at 6-hourly; not offered now — add a 10 d stop later if asked, drawn 6-hourly beyond day 7 with a second, fainter step change.
Slot rule (unchanged): under 4 px per hour the whole chart aggregates to 3-hourly — iPhone portrait 7 d is 3-hourly throughout; web desktop and iPhone landscape keep HRRR hourly up to the seam. Either way the seam is drawn and the NBM treatment applies beyond it.
Honesty, trust line, watch, payload
Trust line names both runs: as of 09:00 · HRRR 15Z + NBM 13Z · ends Tue 9am. Staleness per model: stale NBM desaturates only the NBM region and ambers only its own run label — HRRR 15Z + NBM Tue 13Z. NBM missing: 7 d still works, the chart stops at the HRRR edge, and the right-hand seam label reads NBM unavailable. Never pad.
HRRR missing but NBM present (rare; HRRR outage): show NBM from now, label the left edge NBM · HRRR unavailable, trust line amber. The hero still shows a number; the label says where it came from.
Watch and complications: HRRR only, unchanged. The 12-hour strip and every complication read the HRRR series; when HRRR's horizon is nearer than 12 h the strip ends early (already designed) rather than filling with NBM. Two seconds on a wrist is for the model that resolves the bay.
Payload. hours[] stays HRRR. Add extended: {model:"NBM", run:"13Z", fetched, step:3, from:<hrrr.horizon+1>, blocks:[{t, kn, gust, dirFrom}]}. The server enforces from; clients never filter. The seam is explicit, not inferred.
Colour note. The NBM tag blue (#9BB7D4) is the existing link colour, deliberately not a Beaufort step.
NBM values on the 7-day boards are illustrative (SW front Saturday, NW clearing Sunday, light Monday). The HRRR portion is the real Oct 21 payload.
