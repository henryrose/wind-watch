Sunrise vs sunset — four marker treatments on the same fragment (Wed 3pm → Thu 9am: sunset 6:12pm, sunrise 7:40am)
1 · Filled vs hollow (recommended)
Sunrise is a filled dot, sunset a ring. "The sun is on after it rises, off after it sets." One mark, one colour, lightness-coded — passes the no-hue-alone rule. Already sits exactly where the day shading starts and stops, so the two cues agree.
2 · Half-disc on the horizon
Rise = upper half above the line, set = lower half below it: the literal picture of a sun on a horizon. Reads well at 10 px, less well at the complication's 5 px, and the little horizon line adds ink.
3 · Dot + chevron
Filled dot with an up-chevron for rise, down-chevron for set. Explicit, but it stacks two marks and the chevron collides with the "now" / seam labels in the same band.
4 · Status quo
Both filled. The shading already implies which is which (dark follows the sunset dot), but you have to look at the shading to know — and on the complication strip there is no shading.
